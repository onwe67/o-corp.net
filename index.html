Meet the Founder

Owen is a 13-year-old founder with a big imagination and an even bigger stationery budget.

He started O-CORP as a greeting card company, but he quickly realized that every great company needs a dramatic mission and a very serious logo.

Now he spends his days making cards, dreaming up clever ideas, and trying not to laugh during important meetings.
